A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before you settle in.

Are these videos?

The five scenes shown here use artwork with small animated elements. In the Android app, the snow, bubbles, fireflies and game are drawn in code. You can also import your own silent video wallpapers.

Does it need an account or internet?

The current Android beta works offline. No account, notification access, ads or analytics. Your imported wallpapers and favourites stay on your device.

How much battery does it use?

Motion needs some energy. Mopixy reduces work by keeping artwork cached, animating small elements, and pausing when hidden or in Battery Saver. Exact battery use depends on your device and settings; we don't promise a fixed saving.

Will it work on my lock screen?

Android controls where live wallpapers can be applied. The options available in the system wallpaper picker vary by phone. The previews here let you explore the look before trying it on your device.

MEET THE MAKER

A small start.
A world of possibilities.

Mopixy is an independent Android app created by Md Bashshaaruddin, a computer science student and developer based in India. It began with a simple idea: make a phone's wallpaper feel like a small, living pixel world.

Today, Mopixy is a working Android beta with procedural animation, personal wallpaper imports and local customisation. The interactive scenes on this website let you try its visual style before downloading the app.

Next, we're exploring a scene-design assistant using Claude to help translate creators' ideas into structured animation plans. This is a planned direction; the current Android beta runs offline and does not use Claude or an AI API.
